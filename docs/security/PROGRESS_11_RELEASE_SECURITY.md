# Progress 11 Release Security and Privacy Gates

Release qualification fails closed for missing or stale evidence, cross-tenant/project references, mutable evidence paths, unsigned or incomplete manifests, prohibited provider/model state, restricted-data leakage, authority escalation, consent propagation failure, malicious archive acceptance, failed backup/restore, failed migration, or unreviewed critical vulnerability.

No release campaign may use customer data or human-subject data without a separately authorized and documented-consent workflow. Generated content remains labeled; conflicting recollections remain preserved; proxy and design geometry cannot become authoritative measurements.

## Governing posture

These controls do not authorize production. Progress 11 remains a development milestone, production is **NO-GO**, and Progress 12 is unauthorized.
