# Privacy and Consent

## Data minimization

Capture, index, transmit, and retain only data required for the approved purpose. Restricted spatial regions, faces, voices, private documents, critical-infrastructure details, and family records can be more sensitive than their enclosing project.

Telemetry uses controlled labels and pseudonymous tenant/project context. Raw media, names, facility labels, tokens, prompts, transcript text, and exact coordinates do not belong in metrics.

## Authorization evaluation

Every decision evaluates current:

- identity and workload identity;
- tenant and project role;
- purpose;
- audience;
- classification;
- spatial restriction;
- consent scope and derivative policy;
- retention/legal hold;
- provider/model transfer policy.

Cached UI state and previously delivered notification links do not replace that evaluation.

## Consent lifecycle

A grant records who granted it, for whom, for which purpose/audience/scope, derivative permissions, expiration, and guardian/executor context where applicable. Revocation is auditable and propagates to derivatives and indexes.

Deletion is not always immediate when legal hold, backup retention, shared evidence, or audit obligations apply. The system exposes dependency analysis and residual backup deadlines rather than claiming erasure that has not occurred. Cryptographic erasure requires destruction of the relevant wrapped keys and evidence of the key version affected.

## LiveForever-specific safeguards

- Recollection and fact remain distinct.
- Generated reconstruction and first-person simulation remain distinct from testimony.
- Voice, likeness, dialogue, and presence are off by default.
- Living third parties and minors receive additional protection.
- Family/public editions are independently policy-filtered.
- Preservation export includes consent summary and labels.

## Construction-specific safeguards

Building-system details may pose safety or critical-infrastructure risk. Redact or restrict exact topology, credentials, panel programming, device addresses, and security sequences according to project policy. Export recipients receive only the approved derivative.
