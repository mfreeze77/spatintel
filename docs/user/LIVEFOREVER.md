# LiveForever User Guide

## Truth labels

LiveForever keeps these categories separate:

- direct capture or record;
- a person's recollection;
- corroborated assertion;
- inference;
- generated reconstruction;
- disputed or conflicting recollection.

Generated media is visibly labeled and carries input, model, prompt, output, and review lineage. A vivid reconstruction is not historical fact.

## People, places, and stories

Stories may attach to people, relationships, places, objects, events, media, transcript segments, timestamps, and spatial anchors. Multiple accounts of the same event remain parallel assertions with their own sources and confidence.

## Consent and audiences

A consent grant states subject, grantor, purpose, audience, scope, derivative policy, expiration, and revocation. Private, family, and public editions are generated from current server-side policy.

Revocation can remove access to source and derivative records, indexes, cached editions, generated reconstructions, and exports. Audit retains that revocation happened without making the content visible.

Living third parties, minors, guardians, executors, and inheritance require purpose-specific review. Client-side hiding is never treated as authorization.

## Generated presence safeguards

Voice, likeness, dialogue, and first-person simulation are disabled by default. They require explicit consent and safety approval. Global and subject-level kill switches override a prior grant.

Every immersive experience provides:

- quiet mode;
- pause and reset;
- safe exit;
- reduced motion and safe navigation;
- captions/transcript alternatives;
- clear generated-content labels.

## Preservation

The preservation package is usable without a proprietary viewer and retains originals, transcript/media relationships, scene/graph state, labels, consent summary, and checksums.

Run the synthetic workflow:

```bash
make demo-liveforever
```
