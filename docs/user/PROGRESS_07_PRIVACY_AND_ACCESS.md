# Privacy rights and privileged access

Progress 07 adds governed privacy-inventory, access, deletion, correction, restriction, portability, incident, and privileged-access workflows.

Privileged access is temporary, purpose bound, independently approved, phishing-resistant-MFA bound, auditable, and revocable. A project role alone does not create privileged access.

Privacy-rights requests verify the requester, retain the exact scope and due date, and record outcomes across canonical records, derivatives, indexes, caches, exports, and backup handling. Legal holds and consent controls remain authoritative. Production use requires external privacy and legal review.
