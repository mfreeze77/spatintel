# Progress 11 Support and Escalation

Development-checkpoint support is owned by SIP Engineering. Escalation paths are security, privacy, recovery, release governance, Construction domain review, and LiveForever consent review. Raw-data access is disabled by default. Support artifacts are metadata-first, scoped to tenant/project/purpose, time-bound, checksum-complete, and independently verifiable.

A release incident must preserve source, evidence, audit, failure, rollback, and support lineage. Authority, consent, legal hold, restricted-data, or evidence-integrity incidents block release and cannot be waived.

## Release posture

Progress 11 is a development checkpoint only. Production promotion remains **NO-GO**, and Progress 12 remains unauthorized until a separate True North decision.
